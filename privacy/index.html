DeckCram privacy policy

Publisher: Moojid Hamid. Effective date: September 17, 2026.

DeckCram stores your flashcards, collections, study history, progress, and preferences locally on your device. No account is required. The app does not send this information to a developer-operated server and does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

When you choose to import a file, DeckCram reads the selected file. When you export or share, the selected destination or recipient receives the file you choose to send. Shared .flashlearn collections contain content only; full .flashlearnbackup files include study progress and preferences. Keep backups private when they contain personal information. The app creates local safety backups before replacing data during a restore.

Opening an external link takes you to another website or application. That service has its own privacy practices. Operating-system backups, file storage providers, and sharing destinations are controlled by your device settings; DeckCram does not operate its own cloud-sync service.

You control your local content through the app. Deleting a card everywhere also removes its associated study state; removing it from one set does not. Copies in exported files and safety backups may remain. To remove all device data, follow your operating system's app-data deletion procedure and separately remove exported files/backups you no longer want. Keep an independent backup before uninstalling if you want to retain your library.

For privacy questions, email moojid@muliamation.com.

If you email support, your email address and the information you provide are used to respond to your request. Please avoid sending sensitive study content.

These support pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information under its own privacy statement. This website does not add analytics or advertising trackers.
