How to import

  1. Download a book and save the .flashlearn file to your device.
  2. In DeckCram, open Library and choose Import, then select the file.
  3. Review the preview and confirm. If you already have an earlier revision, choose Update to keep your study progress.

Once imported, the books work offline. These are adapted study aids, not the original source PDFs.
