Form I and Form II

50 complete study tables · Revision 3

Past/present: he (3rd-person masculine singular); command: you (masculine singular). Present forms are indicative. Nouns are citation forms. “— (intransitive sense)” means no ordinary personal passive is taught for this meaning; it does not rule out specialized impersonal constructions. † requires an appropriate complement or preposition. * identifies a regular place/time formation for pattern practice, not a claim that the word is commonly used with that meaning.
