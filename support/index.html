DeckCram support

Public support: moojid@muliamation.com.

Getting started

Open Library to create a book, a set, and cards, or import a .flashlearn file. Study opens by default. Review due cards or practice a collection to learn new cards. Reveal the answer and select Again, Hard, Good, or Easy. Results shows your current progress. Help in the app bar explains the complete workflow.

Reading settings

Settings → Reading comfort offers Standard, Large, and Extra large study text. Settings → Appearance selects the light, dark, or device theme. Device text scaling also applies.

Keep a backup

Settings → Export full backup saves content, progress, and preferences in a .flashlearnbackup. Sharing a book or set creates a .flashlearn content file; it is not a complete backup. Restoring replaces local data after confirmation and creates a safety backup. Never uninstall to troubleshoot before exporting a full backup of data you want to keep.

Reporting a problem

Include the app version, device/OS, action that failed, and error text. Do not send private study files or backups unless you choose to share them. Contact: moojid@muliamation.com.
